WHO WE ARE
*Wakulima Maendeleo Group* is a community-based farmers’ group committed to empowering smallholder farmers through collective action, sustainable agriculture, financial inclusion, and community development.
We bring farmers together to share knowledge, access opportunities, strengthen savings and lending practices, improve agricultural productivity, and build more resilient livelihoods.
Our approach is rooted in the belief that when farmers work together, they can overcome challenges, access better opportunities, and create lasting economic and social change within their communities.
We work with farmers across rural communities, supporting them to strengthen their agricultural practices, improve household incomes, increase food security, and invest in the future of their families and communities.
 Our Vision
A prosperous and resilient farming community where farmers have the knowledge, resources, and opportunities to improve their livelihoods and build a better future.
 Our Mission
To empower smallholder farmers through collective action, sustainable agricultural practices, savings and financial opportunities, knowledge sharing, and community-led development.
 What We Do
* Promote sustainable and productive farming practices.
* Mobilize farmers to work together through collective action.
* Support savings, lending, and financial empowerment.
* Facilitate knowledge and experience sharing among farmers.
* Promote food security and improved household livelihoods.
* Connect farmers with development opportunities and stakeholders.
* Support community initiatives that contribute to long-term rural development.
